UI Tips

Clicking a slot will remove the red border around it.
The randomizer will no longer generate that slot.

Hovering over a slot will reveal two buttons :

- Dice button that rerolls that slot only

- Chain button that goes back in the unlock chain
of that weapon in case you do not have that variant
unlocked (example : Pressing that button on a winfield
silencer would change that slot to a regular winfield).
